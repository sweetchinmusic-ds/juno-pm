## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need evidence-based prioritization. Juno turns noisy Slack, tickets, and strategy docs into a ranked, cited backlog they can defend — the PM team is the bottleneck and there's zero headcount to throw at synthesizing it manually.

## Solution overview

**Retrieval strategy:** Hybrid

PMs ask both kinds of questions. Specific: "What did ROCKET-3421 cite as the blocker?" - needs keyword retrieval. Vibes: "What is the dominant frustration theme this week?" - needs semantic. Going RAG-only sacrifices narrative; long-context only sacrifices precision and budget.

## Retrieval requirements (RAG)

- **Sources:** Slack #escalations (threads tagged P0/P1, last 90 days) + Notion Product workspace (PRDs, strategy docs, retrospectives tagged #product, last 12 months) + Jira ROCKET project (all tickets, last 6 months).

The Notion strategy doc is the one true authority - Slack and Jira are evidence in support of it, never a substitute for it.
- **Chunking / indexing:** Hybrid (Semantic + Keyword). Slack by thread (max 800 tokens/chunk), Notion by H2/H3 section (~1000 tokens/chunk), Jira one ticket = one chunk (~600 tokens). pgvector, one namespace per team for tenant isolation.
- **Grounding rule:** Every priority Juno produces (P0-P3 or notRecommended) cites at least one strategy clause AND at least one piece of evidence (Jira ticket ID, Slack permalink). The PRD draft renders citations inline as footnotes the PM can click to verify. Confidence <70% escalates to the PM instead of asserting a ranking.
- **Freshness:** Notion strategy doc: sync on commit (it lives in the team Notion). Slack #escalations: refresh every 15 minutes - P0s need near-real-time awareness. Jira: refresh hourly - acceptable lag for prioritization.

## Requirements

| # | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retrieval quality and latency | Must | Top-K = 10 retrieval segments per prioritization run (reranked from top-20 via Cohere rerank-v3). p95 latency target < 4s end-to-end (from "Process" click to ranked PRD draft). At ~$0.02-0.04/1k token blended cost, this lands at $0.05-0.10 per Juno run - cheap enough for daily PM use; instrument actual spend before scaling past one team. |
| 2 | Fail-safe on empty retrieval | Must | If retrieval returns < 3 relevant segments OR if no strategy doc is loaded, Juno does NOT produce a P0/P1 ranking. Instead it returns a clear banner: "Insufficient evidence to recommend priority - load a strategy document or escalate to PM judgement." This is a feature, not a failure. |
| 3 | Grounded trust | Must | Every priority Juno produces (P0-P3 or notRecommended) cites at least one strategy clause AND at least one piece of evidence (Jira ticket ID, Slack permalink). The PRD draft renders citations inline as footnotes the PM can click to verify. |
| 4 | Confidence-gated escalation | Must | Confidence <70% escalates with reasoning ("Low confidence (62%). Suggest @pm-oncall review before acting.") instead of asserting a ranking. |
| 5 | Per-team tenant isolation | Must | Team A cannot retrieve Team B's Slack/Jira/Notion data; enforced via per-team pgvector namespace, not a post-hoc filter. |

## Out of scope

Decisions that cannot be cited to a source in the knowledge base. Anything Juno is not allowed to retrieve or act on without a human. Ranking a request when no strategy document is loaded - PM judgment takes over instead. Write access to Jira or Notion (V1 is read-only). Customer contracts, ARR data, and legal docs are not indexed.
