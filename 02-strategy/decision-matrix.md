# AI Solution Decision Matrix · Juno

## The decision

We must decide whether RocketShip builds Automated Prioritization in Juno as a Hybrid Copilot, buys a generic LLM API, or fine-tunes a model. Why now: Roadmap discussions are driven by the loudest executive escalations in Slack rather than customer evidence causing engineering priorities to reverse weekly.

## Options scored

| Option | Cost | Speed | Control | Moat | Risk | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Build | 2 | 2 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 3.6 |
| Buy / API | 5 | 5 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 2.8 |
| Fine-tune | 3 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3.2 |

## Recommendation

Build. It scores highest because Control and Moat are the axes that matter for a ranking system leadership will trust. A generic Buy/API is cheaper and faster but cannot cite RocketShip sources recreating the loudest voice problem. Fine-tune is slower than we can wait and still needs the live corpus. Autonomy stays Copilot: Juno drafts the ranked backlog with citations and the PM approves before publish.
